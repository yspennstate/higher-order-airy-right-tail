# The right tail of higher-order stochastic Airy operators and edges of unitary ensembles

Yitzchak Shmalo

Krishnapur, Rider and Virág proposed a random Schrödinger operator S_{β,k} on the half-line as the scaling limit of
β-ensembles at a soft edge where the equilibrium density vanishes to the order 2k+1/2. We show that the right tail of
minus its lowest eigenvalue decays like exp(-c s^{(4k+3)/(4k+2)}), with the exact constant given by a weighted
Gagliardo–Nirenberg inequality; the exponent was known from a lower bound of Baslingker. For unitary ensembles the
largest eigenvalue at such an edge has a much lighter tail, at most of order exp(-c s^{2k+3/2}), whenever the limiting
edge kernel decays accordingly, so the proposed limit fails for every scale constant. Baslingker proved this for k=1.
For every k ≥ 2 it applies to an even polynomial potential studied by Dai, Du and Lu, whose edge kernel is a dilation
of the kernel of Claeys, Its and Krasovsky, so the conjecture fails there.
